2Quantum Threat & Timeline Deficit

Under Michele Mosca's Theorem (X + Y > Z), if the migration duration (X) plus the required data shelf-life (Y) exceeds the estimated time to a Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computer (Z), assets fall into an active security deficit.

Quantum Vulnerability Vector: Public-key primitives such as RSA and ECC are fundamentally vulnerable to Shor's algorithm running on a future cryptographically-relevant quantum computer (CRQC). Furthermore, ciphertext captured today remains susceptible to Harvest-Now-Decrypt-Later (HNDL) attacks, making timely post-quantum migration an urgent enterprise imperative.
